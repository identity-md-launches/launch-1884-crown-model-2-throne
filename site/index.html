HOW THE THRONE WORKS

Earn the crown.
Leave the history intact.

01

One immortal token

The registry binds the uppercase ticker to its token once. Dethroning never replaces the token, changes supply, or moves the canonical pool.

02

A contest of conviction

Bond 0.01 ETH to name a new beneficiary. Stake ETH on either side for 72 hours. The challenger needs strictly more stake and at least 0.05 ETH total participation. Ties and missed quorum defend the throne.

03

The next reign begins

After a further 24 hours, anyone can finalize. A win redirects future fees; earned fees remain claimable. Losers recover 80% as a pool, with half the 20% slash rewarding winners and half funding the fixed treasury.

The demo’s minimum reign is zero; the production default is seven days. Failed challenges impose a three-day cooldown. Integer rounding goes to the treasury after every staker claims; if no winning stake exists, the full slash goes to the treasury. No registry administrator can override a result.
